## 6. Implementation readings

1. No FX conversion: every window instrument is USD-quoted (6B, 6M; commodities), so t2022.Mkt receives an empty EUR/GBP frame
2. D2: the A9 rolling statistics use each market's history from 1977 up to the window end; issues are counted from the window's first execution to the execution after 1999-12 (the exit of December's positions); a unit break is a non-reversing same-contract move above 30% whose factor is within 2% of a power of ten (a price-unit rescaling); other such moves are market moves and are listed
3. Window check (Amendment 2): t2022.build_targets alone (no Sleeve) for each full sleeve over its window and the month before; at least 6 active in every window month and fewer than 6 in the month before
4. T22 half universes (context): target months with fewer than 6 active instruments hold nothing (section 4.5); the full T22 sleeve has at least 6 in every window month, so nothing is zeroed there
5. T21: the 12 holding months are measured between consecutive decision dates (the twelve months to t, settlements up to and including t), from the chained F1 and F2 indices
6. The 10% single-market risk cap of section 2.4 (a sleeve-construction convention) is applied to all three sleeves, measured with each sleeve's own sigma

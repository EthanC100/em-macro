## Sources and notes by factor

- **y10** — BR: monthly mean of the project's daily 10y series (see results/coverage_report.md); MX: monthly mean of the project's daily 10y series (see results/coverage_report.md); ZA: monthly mean of the project's daily 10y series (see results/coverage_report.md); PL: monthly mean of the project's daily 10y series (see results/coverage_report.md); TR: monthly mean of the project's daily 10y series (see results/coverage_report.md); FR: ECB IRS, EMU convergence 10y yield (monthly average); IT: ECB IRS, EMU convergence 10y yield (monthly average); ES: ECB IRS, EMU convergence 10y yield (monthly average); DE: ECB IRS, EMU convergence 10y yield (monthly average); GB: OECD MEI long-term interest rate IRLT (monthly average); CO: OECD MEI long-term interest rate IRLT (monthly average); CL: OECD MEI long-term interest rate IRLT (monthly average); ID: OECD MEI long-term interest rate IRLT (monthly average)
- **spread_vs_de_bp** — y10 minus Germany y10 (ECB IRS)
- **spread_vs_us_bp** — y10 minus US 10y (FRED DGS10, monthly mean)
- **spread_bench_bp** — vs Germany for FR, IT, ES, GB, PL; vs US for BR, MX, ZA, TR, ID, CO, CL, DE
- **fx_per_usd_avg** — BRL/MXN/ZAR: FRED H.10; TRY/IDR/PLN: Yahoo Finance; COP: Banco de la Republica TRM (datos.gov.co); CLP: Banco Central de Chile dolar observado (mindicador.cl); EUR, GBP: FRED DEXUSEU/DEXUSUK inverted (FR/IT/ES/DE share EUR) *Note:* COP and CLP use official fixings because Yahoo's COP history has bad prints (monthly-change correlation with the TRM only 0.91). EUR and GBP rows are identical across the euro countries.
- **fx_per_usd_end** — BRL/MXN/ZAR: FRED H.10; TRY/IDR/PLN: Yahoo Finance; COP: Banco de la Republica TRM (datos.gov.co); CLP: Banco Central de Chile dolar observado (mindicador.cl); EUR, GBP: FRED DEXUSEU/DEXUSUK inverted (FR/IT/ES/DE share EUR); last observation in month
- **fx_ret_vs_usd_pct** — -100*dlog(month-end local per USD); + = local currency up
- **policy_rate** — BIS WS_CBPOL daily, monthly mean; euro countries = BIS euro-area series (XM), which follows the ECB MRO rate and later the deposit facility rate -- both ECB rates are also in the panel as ecb_mro_rate / ecb_deposit_rate
- **cpi_yoy** — BIS WS_LONG_CPI national CPI, y/y %
- **real_policy_rate** — policy_rate minus cpi_yoy (ex post, same month)
- **reserves_usd_m** — IMF IFS total reserves excl. gold via FRED TRESEG<cc>M052N (monthly) *Note:* Colombia and Chile are not on FRED; see reserves_months_imports (annual) for them.
- **fiscal_balance_gdp** — IMF WEO GGXCNL_NGDP, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **primary_balance_gdp** — IMF WEO GGXONLB_NGDP, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **gross_debt_gdp** — IMF WEO GGXWDG_NGDP, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months) *Note:* WEO latest vintage (country update dates: BR 9/24/2026, MX 9/24/2026, FR 9/25/2026, ZA 9/28/2026, TR 9/25/2026, ID 9/24/2026, PL 9/24/2026, IT 9/24/2026, ES 9/25/2026, GB 9/30/2026, CO 9/29/2026, CL 9/29/2026, DE 9/30/2026). Real-time vintages (what was known at each date) are not included: imf.org WEO vintage files were rate-limited (HTTP 429) during the build.
- **debt_change_1y_pp** — gross_debt_gdp(t) - gross_debt_gdp(t-1), WEO latest vintage
- **debt_change_next5y_pp** — gross_debt_gdp(t+5) - gross_debt_gdp(t), WEO latest vintage: realised history + current projections *Note:* Ex post: for past years this is the realised 5-year change; for recent years it mixes outturns with the latest projections (to 2031). Do not use as an ex-ante expectation.
- **net_debt_gdp** — IMF WEO GGXWDN_NGDP, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **current_account_gdp** — IMF WEO BCA_NGDPD, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **real_gdp_growth** — IMF WEO NGDP_RPCH, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **inflation_weo** — IMF WEO PCPIPCH, latest vintage (annual, assigned to calendar-year months)
- **wgi_va** — World Bank WGI VA.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **wgi_pv** — World Bank WGI PV.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **wgi_ge** — World Bank WGI GE.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **wgi_rq** — World Bank WGI RQ.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **wgi_rl** — World Bank WGI RL.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **wgi_cc** — World Bank WGI CC.EST (annual estimate, -2.5..2.5)
- **reserves_months_imports** — World Bank FI.RES.TOTL.MO (annual)
- **reserves_usd_annual** — World Bank FI.RES.TOTL.CD (annual)
- **cbie_index** — data/external/structural/cbi.csv (see cbi_SOURCE.md)
- **garriga_lvau** — data/external/structural/cbi.csv (see cbi_SOURCE.md)
- **garriga_lvaw** — data/external/structural/cbi.csv (see cbi_SOURCE.md)
- **vdem_v2x_jucon** — data/external/structural/vdem.csv (V-Dem; see vdem_SOURCE.md)
- **vdem_v2juhcind** — data/external/structural/vdem.csv (V-Dem; see vdem_SOURCE.md)
- **vdem_v2juncind** — data/external/structural/vdem.csv (V-Dem; see vdem_SOURCE.md)
- **vdem_v2x_rule** — data/external/structural/vdem.csv (V-Dem; see vdem_SOURCE.md)
- **vdem_v2x_corr** — data/external/structural/vdem.csv (V-Dem; see vdem_SOURCE.md)
- **debt_foreign_share_at** — Arslanalp-Tsuda sovereign investor base (IMF, 2016 vintage, to 2016Q2); % of general/central government debt; data/external/structural/debt_holders.csv *Note:* Levels differ across sources (e.g. GBR 2010Q4: 26.2% A-T vs 16.8% QPSD); never splice them. Euro-area 'foreign' includes other euro residents.
- **debt_foreign_share_qpsd** — World Bank/IMF Quarterly Public Sector Debt (API source 20); % of general/central government debt; data/external/structural/debt_holders.csv
- **debt_foreign_share_eurostat** — Eurostat gov_10q_ggdebt (quarterly) / gov_10dd_ggd (annual); % of general/central government debt; data/external/structural/debt_holders.csv
- **debt_fx_share_qpsd** — World Bank/IMF Quarterly Public Sector Debt (API source 20); % of general/central government debt; data/external/structural/debt_holders.csv
- **debt_fx_share_eurostat** — Eurostat gov_10q_ggdebt (quarterly) / gov_10dd_ggd (annual); % of general/central government debt; data/external/structural/debt_holders.csv
- **inflation_target** — data/external/structural/inflation_targets.csv (central bank sources); point target, else band midpoint (e.g. SARB 3-6% -> 4.5), else ceiling (ECB 'below but close to 2%' before Jul 2021 -> 2.0)
- **inflation_target_low** — lower tolerance band (blank where none, e.g. UK, ECB)
- **inflation_target_high** — upper tolerance band / ceiling
- **imf_programme** — data/external/structural/imf_programmes.csv (IMF lending history)
- **imf_facility** — n/a
- **rating_sp** — S&P long-term FC sovereign rating, month-end, numeric (AAA/Aaa=21 ... C=1); data/events/raw/rating_actions_*.csv
- **rating_moodys** — Moody's long-term FC sovereign rating, month-end, numeric (AAA/Aaa=21 ... C=1); data/events/raw/rating_actions_*.csv
- **rating_fitch** — Fitch long-term FC sovereign rating, month-end, numeric (AAA/Aaa=21 ... C=1); data/events/raw/rating_actions_*.csv
- **rating_avg_outlook_adj** — mean of the three agencies' numeric ratings incl. outlook (+/-1/3, watch +/-2/3)
- **rating_upgrades_n** — number of upgrades across S&P/Moody's/Fitch in the month
- **rating_downgrades_n** — number of downgrades across S&P/Moody's/Fitch in the month
- **gov_seat_share** — lower-house seat share (%) of parties holding cabinet posts at cabinet start; data/external/politics/cabinets_*.csv (ParlGov + Wikipedia/official extensions; presidential systems: governing coalition, see NOTES files) *Note:* Seat counts are at cabinet start (not updated for defections). Turkey after 2018: People's Alliance; France 2017-2020: ParlGov counts LR in Philippe II/Castex (see politics NOTES).
- **gov_n_parties** — number of parties holding cabinet posts (coalition size)
- **gov_minority** — 1 if gov_seat_share < 50
- **n_governments_prior5y** — cabinets/government spells starting in the 5 years up to month end
- **head_party_lr_parlgov** — ParlGov expert-survey left-right, 0-10; party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year
- **gov_parties_lr_parlgov** — ParlGov expert-survey left-right, 0-10; unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_lr_econ_ches** — Chapel Hill Expert Survey economic left-right, 0-10; party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year
- **gov_parties_lr_econ_ches** — Chapel Hill Expert Survey economic left-right, 0-10; unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_lr_econ_vparty** — V-Party v2pariglef economic left-right (higher = right); party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year *Note:* V-Party (v2) ends with 2019 elections; later months carry forward each party's latest scored election. - **gov_parties_lr_econ_vparty** — V-Party v2pariglef economic left-right (higher = right); unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_lr_econ_gps** — Global Party Survey 2019 economic left-right; party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year
- **gov_parties_lr_econ_gps** — Global Party Survey 2019 economic left-right; unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_populism_vparty** — V-Party v2xpa_popul populism index, 0-1; party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year
- **gov_parties_populism_vparty** — V-Party v2xpa_popul populism index, 0-1; unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_populism_gps** — Global Party Survey 2019 populism; party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year
- **gov_parties_populism_gps** — Global Party Survey 2019 populism; unweighted mean over cabinet parties
- **head_party_populist_popu_list** — PopuList populist flag (Europe only); party of the head of government; latest reference election <= year

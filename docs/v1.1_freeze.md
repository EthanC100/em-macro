## The strategy

**v1.1 is v1.0 with one change.** Everything that decides v1.0's targets is v1.0's frozen code, imported from
`em_macro/systematic.py` and never modified:
- the two books (10 currencies against the US dollar; 13 bond positions), as in `docs/v1.0_freeze.md`;
- the carry, trend and value signals, z-scored within each book and averaged equally;
- long the top third and short the bottom third of each book, sized by inverse 36-month volatility;
- each book scaled to 10% volatility, mixed 50/50, the total rescaled to 10%, and the 6× gross-leverage cap;
- the cost model (0.1% of notional traded, 0.3% for Turkey, Indonesia, Colombia and Chile, spread trades twice)
  and the execution lags (FX t+1, bonds t+2).

**The one change (T18, as pre-registered).** v1.0's 25% relative no-trade band is replaced by the spec's two
rebalancing rules together, with the spec's values:

| rule | definition |
|---|---|
| no-trade band in risk units | trade only if \|Δw_i\| · σ_i > 0.1 × the position's risk budget |
| partial rebalancing | w_new = w + 0.5 (w* − w) |

- **Targets w\*:** v1.0's weights after the 10% total rescaling and the 6× cap, which are v1.0's own pre-band
  targets.
- **σ_i:** v1.0's 36-month volatility.
- **Risk budget:** the equal risk v1.0 gives each selected position of its book: the mean of |w\*_i| σ_i over the
  book's selected instruments.
- **Where the band applies:** to the proposed half-trade. The residual of an exited position (target 0) is closed
  once its half-trade falls inside the band.

**Implementation.** `em_macro/systematic_v11.py` imports v1.0's frozen `systematic.py` and applies the rule with
T18's own committed code, `em_macro/t18_rebal.py` (`run` = `capped_targets` → `apply_rules` → `pnl`), unchanged
since the T18 run. `run()` in `systematic_v11.py` is exactly `t18_rebal.run()` (asserted in
`tests/test_v11.py`). Both trial parameters are asserted equal to the pre-registered values (band 0.10, partial
0.5). No parameter of v1.1 was estimated from returns.

**What is not in v1.1.** There is no political-risk overlay and there are no manual overrides: those apply to v1.0's
book (`overrides.yaml`), and v1.0's overlay rule is "no change" in any case. No other trial is combined with T18:
any combination would be a new pre-registered trial (`results/T16-T19_risk_batch.md` §8).

## v1.0 remains the frozen control; both run live in parallel

- **v1.0 is not modified.** No v1.0 file was edited: `em_macro/systematic.py`, `results/systematic/fit/frozen_design.json`, `results/systematic/test/*`
  and v1.0's monthly outputs under `results/systematic/` have the same hashes and are written by the same
  workflow steps as before. `python -m em_macro.verify_v1` still reproduces v1.0 exactly (below).
- **Same inputs, separate records.** Each monthly run computes both versions on the same refreshed inputs:

| | v1.0 (frozen control) | v1.1 (v1.0 + T18) |
|---|---|---|
| code | `em_macro/systematic.py` | `em_macro/systematic_v11.py` (imports the former and `t18_rebal.py`) |
| monthly command | `python -m em_macro.systematic update`, then `python -m em_macro.rebalance` | `python -m em_macro.systematic_v11 update`, then `... rebalance` |
| positions | `results/systematic/positions.csv` | `results/systematic_v1.1/positions.csv` (adds `target_pct_of_capital`, v1.0's pre-band target, and `trade_pct_of_capital`) |
| paper P&L | `results/systematic/paper_pnl.csv` | `results/systematic_v1.1/paper_pnl.csv` (adds `turnover`) |
| rebalance log | `results/systematic/rebalance_log.csv` | `results/systematic_v1.1/rebalance_log.csv` |
| live record from | 3 Nov 2026 rebalance (positions effective 2026-11) | **3 Dec 2026 rebalance (positions effective 2026-12)** |

- **Turnover comparison.** `results/live_compare/turnover.csv` holds both versions' monthly turnover, computed in
  the v1.1 step from the same inputs. v1.0's `paper_pnl.csv` has no turnover column, and v1.0's outputs are not
  changed.
- **Workflow isolation.** The v1.1 steps run after v1.0's update, rebalance log and overlay, and are
  `continue-on-error`. A v1.1 failure cannot stop v1.0's rebalance from being committed, but it still opens or
  comments on the `[ALERT] systematic-monthly failed <date>` issue.
- **Records stay separate.** v1.1's figures are never merged into v1.0's track record, and the reverse. The
  Ledger's Algorithm page shows both side by side: live P&L, Sharpe, drawdown, turnover and months live, over each
  version's own live record and over the common window from December 2026.
- **Path dependence.** Like v1.0's band, v1.1's partial rebalancing depends on the previous month's held position.
  Each monthly run recomputes the whole path from 2005 on the refreshed inputs, as v1.0's update does.
- **Initial files.** `results/systematic_v1.1/positions.csv` and `paper_pnl.csv` were first written on 2026-11-04,
  from the inputs of v1.0's November 2026 run (`bd236f9`). Those inputs reproduce v1.0's committed November
  positions exactly and its paper P&L to within 5e-7 (the files' 6-decimal rounding). They hold v1.1's positions as
  of 2026-10 (effective 2026-11), which are **pre-live**. The first live v1.1 rebalance, and the first row of its
  rebalance log, is the 3 December 2026 run.

## Diagnostic: cost dependence of T18's advantage (not a decision)

This section is **diagnostic only**. It changes nothing in v1.1 or v1.0, and nothing here is a test or a reason to
adopt, drop or re-parameterise either version. T18's decision basis remains 1x v1.0 costs, as pre-registered.

**Method.** `python -m em_macro.systematic_v11 costs` ran v1.0 and v1.1 over 2020-01 to 2026-09 (81 months, all 23
instruments) on the frozen inputs, at several cost levels. Neither version's positions depend on costs: selection,
sizing, vol targeting, the cap and both bands are cost-free. The code asserts that the weights are identical in
every row, so only the cost line changes. The rows are:
- 0.5x, 1x and 2x v1.0's cost vector;
- the cost audit's central published per-instrument estimates (`results/cost_audit/cost_estimates.csv`,
  `central_bp`; turnover-weighted about 5.7bp per unit traded against v1.0's 19.6bp), with and without the audit's
  roll-cost convention (0.5 × one-way cost per roll: FX monthly, bonds quarterly);
- 0x (gross) for context.

**Checks.** The v1.0 rows equal the cost audit's published `step3_sensitivity.csv`, and v1.1's 1x and 2x rows equal
T18's committed `performance.csv` (max abs difference 0.0 at 4 decimals in both cases).

differences: `results/systematic_v1.1/freeze/cost_sensitivity.csv`.

**What it shows:**
- **The advantage grows with cost.** v1.1's Sharpe advantage rises from +0.14 at zero cost to +0.26 at v1.0's
  costs and +0.39 at twice them, because v1.1 trades 39% less.
- **At the central estimates it is smaller.** At the cost audit's central estimates, which are well below v1.0's
  assumptions for the G10 currencies, the Bund and the spreads over the Bund, the advantage is +0.17 (+0.17 with
  rolls), about two thirds of the +0.26 on which T18 passed.
- **About half the advantage does not come from costs.** It is still +0.14 before costs: v1.1's gross return is
  higher (+10.4% against +10.0% a year), its volatility lower (8.8% against 9.6%) and its drawdown smaller.
- **The drawdown improvement holds at every cost level** (2.3 to 5.7 points).
- **The bar was set at 1x, not at the central estimates.** T18's pre-registered criteria were evaluated only at 1x
  (decisive) and 2x (reported). Whether T18 would pass at the central estimates was not pre-registered, is not
  claimed here, and would need its own test.

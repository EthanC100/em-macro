## The strategy

v1.0 trades two books monthly:
- **Currencies:** 10 against the US dollar — BRL, MXN, ZAR, TRY, IDR, PLN, GBP, COP, CLP and EUR.
- **Bonds:** 13 positions — the 10-year local bonds of Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey, Indonesia, Colombia and
  Chile (excess return over the local policy rate); 10-year spreads over the Bund for France, Italy, Spain, the UK and
  Poland; and the Bund itself (excess over the ECB rate).

Each month, three signals are standardised across the instruments of each book:
- **carry:** the rate differential, or yield minus funding rate;
- **trend:** the average of the 12- and 3-month return z-scores;
- **value:** for currencies, the real exchange rate against its 5-year average; for bonds, the sovereign risk
  model's frozen v1 mispricing.

The three z-scores are averaged equally. The top third of each book is bought and the bottom third sold. Each
position is sized by inverse 36-month volatility, each book is scaled to 10% volatility, the books are mixed 50/50,
and the total is rescaled to 10% target volatility, with a 6× gross-leverage cap on targets. A 25% no-trade band
applies.

Costs are 0.1% of notional traded, or 0.3% for Turkey, Indonesia, Colombia and Chile; spread trades pay twice. Bond
positions earn the change between the next two monthly-average yields, as an execution lag. All design choices were
made on 2005–2019 data and frozen before the single test on 2020-01 to 2026-09.

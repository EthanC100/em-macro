# Lab sleeves

One YAML file per sleeve, `L<nnn>.yaml`, in the format documented in `lab/book.py`. A sleeve is frozen once
`python -m lab.book launch L<nnn>` records its SHA-256 in `lab/registry.csv`: to change a sleeve, launch a new one.
Limits: 0.5% paper risk per sleeve, 5% for the whole Lab; retirement by default 6 months after launch.
Sleeves come from explorer nominations (data to 2019-12-31 only) and launch only after The Opposition has written
its case and the sleeve passes the hard rules. The Lab ("Lab - experimental") is never mixed into any other book.
